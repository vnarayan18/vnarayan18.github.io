\section*{Project overview}
A compact robotic-arm development project focused on the full engineering chain: defining reach and payload goals, designing joints and links, selecting actuators, solving kinematics, and validating repeatable motion.

\section*{Engineering problem}
Robotic-arm performance emerges from coupled choices in geometry, torque margin, stiffness, backlash, wiring, control timing, and end-effector requirements.

\section*{System architecture}
\begin{itemize}
\item Mechanical structure: modular links, accessible joints, and replaceable end effector.
\item Actuation: stepper or servo joints chosen from torque-speed and holding requirements.
\item Control: joint-space commands with limits, homing, coordinated interpolation, and stop logic.
\item Software: forward kinematics first, followed by bounded inverse-kinematics experiments.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Engineering focus}
\begin{itemize}
\item Static torque estimates include link mass, payload, lever arm, and safety factor.
\item Workspace and singularity checks guide link dimensions and joint limits.
\item Backlash and structural compliance are treated as measured system properties.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Verification plan}
\begin{itemize}
\item Measure repeatability at multiple workspace points and payload conditions.
\item Check joint temperature, current draw, overshoot, settling, and limit behavior.
\item Run pick-and-place sequences only after homing and emergency-stop tests pass.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Status and evidence boundary}
Summer 2026 robotics design brief; final degrees of freedom and payload remain configuration-dependent.
